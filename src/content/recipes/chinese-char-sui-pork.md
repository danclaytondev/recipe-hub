### Ingredients

- Pork shoulder
- 2 tbsp Char siu sauce
- 1 tbsp Oyster sauce
- ~1 tbsp Soy sauce
- ~1 tbsp Dark soy sauce
- ~1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
- 1 tsp White pepper
- 1 tsp Five spice powder
- 1 tsp Sugar

### Instructions

1. **Make the marinade** — In a bowl, mix together char siu sauce, oyster sauce, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Chinese cooking wine, sugar, white pepper, and five spice powder until well combined.
2. **Prepare the pork** — Use a fork to poke holes all over the pork shoulder to help the marinade soak in. Coat the pork thoroughly with the sauce, making sure every piece is well covered.
3. **Marinate** — Cover and refrigerate ideally for 4 hours or more.
4. **Preheat the air fryer & first cook** — Set air fryer to 200C, pre heat and then cook pork shoulder steaks for ~8 mins.
5. **Flip and baste** — Flip the pork and brush with more char siu sauce or leftover marinade to build that glossy coating. Cook for a couple more minutes.
6. **Final glaze** — Optionally, flip once more and brush with honey or an extra layer of char siu sauce for added shine and sweetness.
